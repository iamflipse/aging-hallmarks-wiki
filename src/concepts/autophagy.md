# Disabled Macroautophagy

*Source: [§6 of the paper](../sources/ijms-27-18-8080-fulltext.md)*

## Mechanisms

- Macroautophagy: conserved lysosomal degradation pathway — cytoplasmic cargo is sequestered in double-membrane autophagosomes, then fused with lysosomes for recycling.
- Core machinery: **ULK1 complex**, class-III **PI3K–VPS34** phagophore nucleation, **ATG12–ATG5–ATG16L1** conjugation, **LC3/GABARAP** lipidation, cargo-receptor engagement, **HOPS/SNARE**-mediated fusion; **TFEB** coordinates lysosomal biogenesis and autophagy gene expression.
- Selective pathways clear **mitochondria (mitophagy)**, aggregates, lipid droplets, pathogens.
- **Autophagic flux** (not a static LC3-II/p62 measurement) is the mechanistically relevant quantity, because autophagosome formation and lysosomal degradation can change independently.

## Evidence

- Loss of core autophagy genes → severe tissue degeneration.
- **Atg5 overexpression** or disruption of **Beclin1–BCL-2** extends mouse lifespan.
- **Caloric restriction, mTORC1 inhibition, exercise, and spermidine** all stimulate autophagy (each also affects other pathways).
- **Urolithin A**: engages mitophagy programs; selected human-trial functional/biomarker effects, **not** proven to modify human aging.
- **NAD+ precursors**: consistent biochemical target engagement, **inconsistent functional benefit**.

## Therapeutic window

- Autophagy can suppress tumor initiation; established tumors **use** it to survive hypoxia and treatment.
- More autophagosome biogenesis doesn't help when lysosomal acidification, hydrolase activity, or membrane fusion is the bottleneck.
- Mitophagy needs coordinated fission + cargo recognition + lysosomal competence.
- Clinical studies must identify the **rate-limiting defect**, quantify pathway flux in the target tissue, and prespecify oncological/immunological safety.
