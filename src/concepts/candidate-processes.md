# Candidate Processes & Omitted Dimensions

*Source: [§14 of the paper](../sources/sensors-8080-fulltext.md)*

The review examines two processes as **candidate additions** to the 12-hallmark framework — deliberately *not* classified as established hallmarks:

## RNA-processing defects

- Plausible candidate: **alternative splicing, intron retention, RNA modifications, RNA surveillance, ribonucleoprotein homeostasis** vary across tissues and generate aberrant isoforms that burden proteostasis.
- Some **splicing factors** affect lifespan in model organisms; **splice-switching oligonucleotides** prove therapeutic tractability in defined genetic diseases.
- Limitation: evidence that generalized correction of RNA processing modifies mammalian aging remains **limited**; RNA dysregulation may be downstream of genomic/epigenomic/proteostatic defects.
- Its **incremental value is post-transcriptional**: isoform choice, RNA surveillance, and RNP homeostasis can be perturbed even when DNA/chromatin are normal.

## Extracellular-matrix (ECM) remodeling

- The ECM adds a **tissue-level dimension**: it stores damage in **long-lived structural components**, shapes mechanotransduction, and constrains regenerative niches.
- **Collagen crosslinking, elastin fragmentation, glycation, fibrosis**, and altered matrix turnover affect vascular compliance, lung elasticity, muscle function, neural plasticity, and stem-cell niches.
- Cells sense stiffness via **integrins, focal adhesions, YAP–TAZ, TGF-β, WNT** → feedback loops among matrix damage, senescence, inflammation, and regenerative failure.
- Because matrix proteins are long-lived, damage accumulates a **cumulative exposure record** not erased by cell replacement.
- Interventions (anti-glycation, anti-fibrosis, mechanosignaling) need **tissue-specific** evaluation since ECM remodeling is also required for repair.

## Criteria for a candidate module

A process earns separate-module status when its definition supports **measurements or interventions with explanatory, predictive, or experimental value** beyond existing modules — not merely because the process appears often in aging datasets.
