# Genomic Instability

*Source: [§2 of the paper](../sources/ijms-27-18-8080-fulltext.md)*

## Mechanisms

- Nuclear and mitochondrial genomes are constantly exposed to replication errors, spontaneous hydrolysis, UV/ionizing radiation, and inflammatory oxidants.
- Repair pathways (base-excision, nucleotide-excision, mismatch repair, homologous recombination, NHEJ, ATM/ATR responses) preserve function — **none is error-free**.
- With age: somatic mutations, structural variants, chromosomal abnormalities, clonal expansions, and persistent DNA-damage foci accumulate in **tissue-specific** patterns.
- Single-cell sequencing shows rising mutation burden across human tissues; **clonal hematopoiesis** and mutant epithelial clones show selection acting on this variation.
- Defects in genome-maintenance genes cause segmental progeroid syndromes; long-lived species show enhanced genome maintenance.

## Key nuance

- Mutation **count** is less important than genomic location, cellular context, clonal behavior, and **damage-induced signaling**.
- Many mutations are neutral; cells tolerate substantial mosaicism.
- Persistent DNA-damage signaling (ATM/ATR/p53/PARP) can drive arrest, senescence, apoptosis, or inflammatory signaling; cytosolic chromatin fragments and derepressed retroelements activate **cGAS-STING** and type-I interferon programs, linking genome instability to inflammation.
- Short-term protection vs late-life cost: damage responses guard against malignant transformation but can propagate tissue dysfunction.

## Target modulation: OGG1

- **OGG1** (8-oxoguanine DNA glycosylase) excises oxidative 8-oxoguanine lesions.
- Activator **TH10785**: boosts OGG1 catalytic activity + β,δ-lyase activity → faster oxidative-lesion repair.
- Inhibitor **TH5487**: blocks OGG1 binding in gene promoters → suppresses proinflammatory transcription; reduces myofibroblast transition and collagen deposition in a murine pulmonary-fibrosis model.
- **Same enzyme, two opposite directions** — the appropriate direction depends on the intended target (repair capacity vs transcriptional signaling).

## Translation caveats

- Separating repair enhancement from generalized suppression of DNA-damage responses is essential; checkpoint attenuation can let oncogenic cells survive and expand.
- Interventions on nucleotide metabolism, NAD+-dependent repair, sirtuins, transposable elements, or cytosolic-DNA sensing may hit multiple hallmarks at once.
- Required evaluation: cancer surveillance, clonal dynamics, tissue-specific mutation spectra, functional outcomes.
