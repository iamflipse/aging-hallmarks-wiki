## The paper's four-question appraisal framework

For each of the 12 hallmarks, the review asks:

1. Does the process change **reproducibly with age** in relevant human tissues?
2. Does **experimental manipulation** alter age-related function or survival (not just an isolated molecular readout)?
3. Is the effect **conserved across biological contexts** (species, tissues, life stages)?
4. Can the process be **measured and modified in humans**?

This appraisal exposes where evidence is strong (e.g., senescence, mitochondrial quality control) and where it is thin (e.g., organism-level efficacy of most geroprotective candidates).
