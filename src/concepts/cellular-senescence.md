# Cellular Senescence

*Source: [§9 of the paper](../sources/ijms-27-18-8080-fulltext.md)*

## The senescent state

- Heterogeneous, context-dependent **stable cell-cycle arrest** in metabolically active cells.
- Triggers: telomere dysfunction, oncogenic signaling, genotoxic/oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, chromatin disruption, cytotoxic therapy.
- Senescent cells acquire the **senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP)** and elevated **SA-β-gal** activity.
- Distinct from quiescence and terminal differentiation; rare re-entry into the cell cycle is possible.
- Core regulators: **p53–p21** and **p16INK4a–RB** axes enforce arrest; **p38 MAPK, mTOR, NF-κB, C/EBPβ, JAK-STAT, cGAS–STING** shape context-dependent features.
- Transient senescence is **protective** (embryogenesis, wound repair, tumor suppression); harm comes from **persistent** senescent cells (formation > immune clearance) and chronic SASP output.

## Markers

- No single marker is specific/universal. Telomere-dysfunction foci in primate dermal fibroblasts and **p16INK4a** in human blood T cells both rise strongly with age.
- Persistent DDR (ATM–CHK2, ATR–CHK1) stabilizes p53/p21; unresolved lesions form γH2AX/53BP1 foci.
- Cytosolic chromatin/mtDNA → **cGAS–STING** → NF-κB inflammation.
- Reliable identification requires a **combination**: proliferative arrest + p16INK4a/p21 + SA-β-gal + lipofuscin/lysosomal expansion + lamin-B1 loss + persistent DNA-damage foci + secretory markers.

## SASP

- Dynamic, nonuniform mix of cytokines, chemokines, growth factors, matrix proteases, bioactive lipids, EVs.
- Common components: **IL-6, CXCL8/IL-8, CCL2, CXCL1, IL-1α** (surface IL-1α activates IL-1R–IRAK1 → NF-κB/C/EBPβ, upstream of the IL-6-CXCL8 network); **IL-1β** (requires inflammasome/caspase-1 maturation, context-specific).
- Persistent SASP: paracrine senescence transmission, ECM/stem-niche alteration, endothelial/metabolic impairment, myeloid activation → chronic systemic inflammation (tissue/disease-dependent).

## Senotherapeutics

- **Senolytics** exploit senescent anti-apoptotic dependencies: BCL-2 family, PI3K-AKT, SRC family, ephrin receptors, p53, HSP90.
- Best studied: **dasatinib + quercetin (D+Q)**, **fisetin**, **navitoclax** (strong preclinical activity but dose-limiting thrombocytopenia).
- cFLIP suppression + DR5 activation sensitizes therapy-induced senescent cancer cells.
