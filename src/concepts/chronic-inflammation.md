# Chronic Inflammation ("Inflammaging")

*Source: [§12 of the paper](../sources/ijms-27-18-8080-fulltext.md)*

## Definition

- **Inflammaging**: chronic, low-grade inflammatory activity in older adults, associated with frailty, disability, cardiovascular events, cancer, morbidity, and mortality.
- Heterogeneous in **sources, cellular composition, anatomical distribution, and temporal course**.
- Sources: senescent cells, visceral adiposity, damaged mitochondria, cytosolic DNA, impaired autophagy, barrier failure, periodontal disease, tissue fibrosis, environmental exposures.
- **Immunosenescence** (impaired adaptive responses, altered myeloid function, clonal hematopoiesis, defective resolution) can coexist with chronic innate activation.
- The state reflects dysregulated **timing, localization, magnitude, and resolution** — not a uniform increase in immunity.

## Pathways

- Pattern-recognition receptors → **NF-κB, JAK-STAT, cGAS-STING, NLRP3** connect upstream damage to inflammatory gene expression and cytokine maturation.
- **NLRP3** requires transcriptional priming + a second signal → inflammasome assembly, **caspase-1**, maturation of **IL-1β/IL-18**.

## Evidence & intervention

- **CANTOS trial**: **IL-1β inhibition** reduces recurrent cardiovascular events in patients with residual inflammatory risk — but **increases fatal infection**. Establishes causal therapeutic effect in a defined disease context (not proof that cytokine blockade slows aging).
- Lifestyle levers: regular exercise, smoking cessation, obesity treatment, sleep, nutrition — reduce inflammatory burden across multiple systems.
- Pharmacological candidates: cytokine antagonists, inflammasome inhibitors, **senotherapeutics** (senolytics eliminate senescent cells; senomorphics attenuate the SASP), mTOR modulators, metabolic/immune drugs.
- Trial design: distinguish inflammatory biomarkers from immune competence; measure **vaccine response, infection rates, wound repair, functional recovery**.

## Framing

Chronic inflammation is a **network state** integrating damage, failed clearance, barrier dysfunction, and altered intercellular communication — best studied as an integrative hallmark module.
