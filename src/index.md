# Hallmarks of Aging — Wiki

> A curated wiki distilling the 2026 *International Journal of Molecular Sciences* review:
> **The Hallmarks of Aging: From Molecular Mechanisms to Clinical Translation** (Chmielewski & Strzelec, IJMS 2026;27(18):8080, open access CC-BY).

The paper appraises the **12 hallmarks of aging** with a **four-question evidence framework**, examines **RNA-processing defects** and **extracellular-matrix remodeling** as candidate processes, operationalizes a **"provisional causal module"** concept, and lays out a staged **clinical-translation** program.
